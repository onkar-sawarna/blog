I spent four years at Menlo Security — starting as an intern, growing into a full-time engineering role, learning most of what I know about production systems the hard way. Five months ago I moved to a startup. Different pace, different problems, same underlying question I keep coming back to: **how do the systems I use every day actually work under the hood?**

This blog is where I'm going to answer that question in public, mostly for myself.

## What this is

A working log, not a highlight reel. Notes on system design concepts as I study them, write-ups of problems I've actually run into, and the occasional post on the less technical side of building a career — the stuff nobody puts in a job description.

## What this isn't

Polished takes with a thesis I decided in advance. If a post changes its mind halfway through, that's the process working, not a mistake I forgot to edit out.
